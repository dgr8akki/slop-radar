Slop Radar

Labels LinkedIn posts as human, unclear or AI slop as you scroll.

It judges writing style, not authorship. No tool can prove a post was AI-written, and a person writing in LinkedIn’s house style will be labelled slop too. Read the labels as a hint, not a verdict on anyone.
